Bigger mod cards: 10 directions

Image-first, square-ish replacements for the catalog's 90 px-cover row card, in the site's Adonis dark tokens and font. Every design uses the same four samples: a wide 16:9 cover, a tall 3:5 cover with no version, no cover (the letter placeholder on the id-hashed accent), and a long title with all five tags and a 4:3 cover. The last tile in each grid is sample 4 forced into its hover / focus state.

The accent-blue Lucide inbox download button stays the primary action everywhere; clicking anywhere else on a card opens the details view. A missing version drops the "v" part of the meta line instead of printing a bare "v".
